Renewi · Vlaardingen pilot

How a person gets onto the Vlaardingen site

Everyone who enters must have confirmed the life-saving rules, and the gate officer must be able to see that before letting them in. People Renewi expects are invited and checked in advance (Path A). People nobody expected register themselves at the entrance (Path B). Both end at the same gate kiosk.

Life-saving rules confirmation

A statement the person ticks themselves. The exact wording, rules version and time are stored, and the gate checks it every time.

Access QR and entry QR

The access QR comes from an invitation (Path A). The entry QR comes from walk-up registration (Path B). Each is read in its own kiosk panel. The entry QR is also emailed after the code check.

Entrance sign

One shared QR at the site entrance. It identifies the site and never a person.

Refusals

Every refusal is stored in the audit trail with who, when and why, whether the system refused or the officer did. A refusal is a record and does not lock the person out.

Reference

An 8-character code that stands in for the entry QR when the phone cannot be scanned.

This describes the working demo. The rules wording is sample text. Renewi still has to supply the approved wording, decide who may use Path B, and decide how long records are kept.
